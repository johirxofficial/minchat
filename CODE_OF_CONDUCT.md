# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MinChat to be a welcoming project. Everyone who participates — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation — deserves a harassment-free experience.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps the project:

- being kind and patient with people who are learning
- giving actionable, specific feedback about code
- accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- respecting different viewpoints and deployment needs
- focusing criticism on the work, not the person

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language
- sexualised language or attention
- publishing someone's private information without permission
- deliberately derailing discussions
- dismissing reports of abuse or security problems
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat and email — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers who are involved in an incident should step away from handling it.
